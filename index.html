    About Us

    Choice Vine Montessori Pre School is a nurturing early-learning haven located in the heart of Kimwanyi-Kira, created to give every child a strong start and a shining path ahead. Rooted in the Montessori philosophy, the school offers a warm, thoughtfully prepared environment where curiosity leads, hands explore, and young minds grow with confidence. Our learning spaces are designed to spark independence, social growth, and a love for discovery, giving children the firm grounding they need to thrive academically and emotionally. At Choice Vine, every child is tended like a young vine ready to flourish, growing into the bright future they were made for.

    Our Mission

    At Choicevine Montessori Pre School, our mission is to provide a child-centered learning environment that nurtures curiosity, independence, and a love for learning. We are committed to guiding each child to reach their full potential through the Montessori philosophy, fostering creativity, confidence, and respect for self, others, and the environment. We strive to build a strong foundation for lifelong learning by encouraging exploration, critical thinking, and positive social values in a safe, supportive, and inclusive community.
